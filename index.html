The Nocturne Company

The Nocturne Company is an interdisciplinary collective of artists and people who simply love music. Some of us know a lot about one thing, most of us know a little about many, and that mix is the point.

We bring curiosity, half-knowledge and experience from other fields to the table and build things together: songs, lyrics, images, sound design, video. We work with digital instruments, samples, AI tools and our own words, and we keep trying out what the next tool can do.

The results range from dance-pop for a wedding to Eurodance for Halloween to schlager at 160 BPM. We have no fixed genre and no business plan.

Stay curious. Try everything. Get it wrong. Make it anyway.
